# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in DAgents welcoming, respectful,
and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity,
experience, or level of contribution.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific when discussing code or product
  decisions.
- Assume good intent, while addressing impact when behavior causes harm.
- Keep feedback focused on the work and the documented project goals.
- Respect private security reports and do not disclose unpatched
  vulnerabilities publicly.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualized
content, and publishing private information without consent are not allowed.
Deliberate disruption of CI, abuse of project infrastructure, or bypassing
security review is also unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report violations privately to the repository maintainers through the security
reporting channel described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or through GitHub's
private contact options. Do not include sensitive details in a public issue.
Maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when necessary to protect the community.

This document is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
